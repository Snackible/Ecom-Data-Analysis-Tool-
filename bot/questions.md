# Ads Bot question bank (111)

Numbering matches the Instruction Manual. Group letters in the manual run A to J; here groups are labelled by name.


## Core

1. Which search query of the keyword had high impressions and higher budget spent, but lower revenue, and dragged ROAS down?
2. Which city is consuming more budget?
3. Which city is giving me the highest ads revenue?
4. Which SKU × city × keyword is working best? Where is there scope for improvement?
5. What changes should I make this week?
6. What is your analysis on this?
7. Which campaigns should I increase budgets on?
8. What is my budget split across attack (competitor keywords), expand (category keywords) and defend (Snackible keywords)?
9. Which keywords should I change match type on?
10. Based on the type of keywords working for me, suggest more for a specific campaign.
11. Should I start a city-wise campaign for any specific SKU?

## Health check

_Good answer: leads with the verdict, gives numbers with week-on-week change, separates what the data proves from guesses, and flags days still collecting attributed sales._

12. Give me a one-line verdict on Instamart ads for the period I just uploaded.
13. How did spend, impressions, clicks, GMV and ROAS change versus last week?
14. Which 3 things changed the most this week, and why?
15. Is ROAS falling because of cost, click-through, conversion or attribution lag?
16. Which days in this period are still collecting attributed sales and shouldn't be judged yet?
17. What % of total spend went to placements without a SKU (Collection Ads, Auto Suggest, Search Inline Banner)?
18. What is A2S for this period, and is it above my ceiling?
19. Which campaigns spent the most with the least to show for it?
20. Which campaigns improved the most versus last week?
21. If I can only do one thing today, what should it be?

## Search query waste

_Good answer: uses the search query report, applies the minimum-data rule, gives the rupee amount at stake, and names the specific fix (negative, exact match, lower bid)._

22. Which search queries spent the most with ROAS below 1?
23. Which search queries spent money but got zero GMV?
24. List the top 20 wasteful search queries with spend, clicks, ROAS and the campaign they came from.
25. Which irrelevant searches am I paying for?
26. Which searches have high click-through but poor conversion?
27. Which searches convert well but get little spend, so deserve more budget?
28. What new search queries appeared this week, and how are they doing?
29. Group my search queries by theme (chips, khakhra, biscuits and dips, puffs, protein, healthy) and rank the themes by ROAS.
30. Which spelling variants should be combined, and what do they look like together?
31. What should my negative keyword list be this week?
32. Which search queries should get their own exact-match keyword?
33. How much spend would I save by cutting every query below my waste threshold?

## Match type

_Good answer: judges each keyword by the searches it actually served on, separates deliberate broad reach from spill, and states the fix with the spend it affects._

34. How do broad and exact match compare on spend, CPC, CPM and ROAS?
35. Which broad keywords are spilling into irrelevant searches?
36. What searches does broad match on "snackible" actually serve on, and what share is truly about the brand?
37. Which broad keywords serve competitor searches by accident?
38. For "healthy snacks", how do exact and broad compare, and should I shift budget between them?
39. Which keywords should move from broad to exact?
40. Which exact keywords are too narrow and getting too few impressions?
41. Where are exact and broad versions of the same keyword competing with each other?
42. How much of "choki choki" broad spend landed on other searches like "wafers" and "dark fantasy"?
43. What share of each keyword's spend lands on searches that don't contain the keyword?
44. Give me a match-type fix list: keyword, problem, fix and expected saving.
45. Which match type works best in each bucket (attack, defend, expand)?

## CPM and cost efficiency

_Good answer: converts eCPM into cost per 1,000 impressions, compares within the same placement, and links cost to outcome, never cost alone._

46. What is my average cost per 1,000 impressions by ad placement?
47. Which search queries have the highest CPM, and are they converting?
48. Which campaigns have high CPM and low click-through?
49. How do CPM-bid and CPC-bid campaigns compare on cost per conversion?
50. Which CPC campaigns are paying more per click than I accept?
51. Is Collection Ads IM at ₹9,000 a day still worth it, given its GMV trend?
52. Which keywords pay a high CPM for a poor ad rank?
53. Where am I paying for top ad rank without a matching return?
54. Which campaigns don't spend their full daily budget, and why might that be?
55. Has CPM gone up this week in any placement or city?
56. Which placements give the cheapest cost per order? (Cost per order uses TOTAL_CONVERSIONS unless told otherwise (confirm before using TOTAL_A2C).)
57. Where should I lower bids, and by how much?

## City

_Good answer: ignores cities below the minimum spend, merges duplicate city names, checks availability before blaming the ads, and says when a search-query-by-city view isn't possible._

58. Rank cities by spend and ROAS. Which ones are below break-even?
59. Which cities have high spend and weak ROAS?
60. Which cities have strong ROAS but get too little spend?
61. Are Bangalore and Mumbai getting the right share of the budget?
62. For a given city, which keywords are wasting the most?
63. Which cities have high CPM compared with the account average?
64. Which cities got worse this week, and is it cost, conversion or availability?
65. Merge "Noida" and "Noida 1" and show me the combined view.
66. Group cities by tier and compare ROAS by tier. (City tiers come from settings.city_tiers (null until filled).)
67. Which cities should I cut, hold or scale this week?

## SKU

_Good answer: uses short SKU names, checks stock before recommending more spend, and flags spend that can't be tied to a SKU._

68. Rank SKUs by spend and ROAS.
69. Which SKUs get heavy spend at weak ROAS?
70. Which SKUs have strong ROAS and room to scale?
71. Which SKUs are advertised on searches that don't match them? (A search does not match a SKU when the query contains none of the words in settings.sku_keywords (ask the user until filled).)
72. For a given SKU, which searches and cities drive its results?
73. Which SKUs have high click-through but low conversion (possible price, pack size or listing issue)?
74. Are any SKUs taking sales from each other?
75. Which SKUs should get more spend, and is there enough stock to support it?
76. Which SKU and city combinations are the most wasteful?
77. Give me a SKU × city × match type grid for my top 5 SKUs.

## Attack, defend, expand

_Good answer: classifies by search query served as well as keyword bid, judges attack on trial and closeness rather than ROAS alone, and never recommends cutting exact-match brand defence lightly._

78. How is spend split across attack, defend and expand this week, versus my target split?
79. What is ROAS for each bucket, judged by the searches actually served on?
80. How much of my defend spend actually landed on brand searches?
81. Which competitor keywords are worth keeping, and which should I cut?
82. How are Too Yum, Troovy, Let's Try and Choki Choki doing as attack keywords?
83. Which competitors seem closest to my products, judging by conversion?
84. Is my brand defence holding its ad rank on brand searches?
85. Is Guaranteed Top Slot on brand searches still efficient?
86. Which expand keywords are my growth engines, and which are burning money?
87. Which category keywords should I add, based on searches that already convert?
88. How much attack spend leaked into non-competitor searches?
89. Give me a scorecard for the three buckets: spend, share, ROAS, trend and action.

## Availability

_Good answer: says clearly when availability data is missing, never counts stock in transit, and links any spend cut to the stock problem._

90. Which campaigns kept spending while availability was poor?
91. Which SKU and city combinations should I pause until fill rate recovers?
92. How much of this week's ROAS drop is likely availability rather than ads?
93. Which placements keep serving even when products are out of stock?
94. If I combine this report with my fill rate data, where am I wasting the most?

## Budget and scaling

_Good answer: ranks actions by rupee impact, judges extra spend by marginal ROAS, gives a measure for each change, and states the downside._

95. I need to cut ₹50,000 this week. Where should it come from?
96. I have an extra ₹50,000. Where should it go?
97. What did the extra spend since I last raised budgets actually bring back, measured on the extra rupees alone?
98. At what spend level does each top keyword stop paying back?
99. How should I phase the rest of this month's budget to hit target without breaking A2S?
100. Which campaigns should I pause, reduce, hold or scale? Give me a table.
101. What are the risks if I follow your recommendations?
102. How will I know next week whether the changes worked?

## Diagnosis, judgement and brainstorming

_Good answer: separates proof from guesswork, admits uncertainty, pushes back where the data supports it, writes leadership updates conclusion first, and in brainstorming gives distinct options with a cheap test for each._

103. Why did ROAS fall this week? Show me the chain of causes. Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
104. Is this a one-off dip or a lasting structural problem? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
105. What looks good in this report but is actually misleading? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
106. Which recommendation are you least sure about, and why? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
107. What data would change your recommendation? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
108. Challenge my decision to raise spend for a higher target. Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
109. Write a 5-line update for my founder on this week's Instamart ads. Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below." Q109 may be drafted as a 5-line template, marked draft for review.
110. What principle should I take away from this week's data? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."
111. Let's brainstorm: how do I grow Instamart ads revenue 30% next month without dropping ROAS below break-even? Judgement question: reply with supporting numbers and the note "Judgement answer needs a human review. Supporting data below."

## Beyond the 111 (flexible questions, examples used for matcher tests)

These are not numbered questions. They show the kinds of free-form asks handled by `bot/flex.py` (lookups, top N, trends, period compares, shares, counts, outliers). See `bot/capabilities.md`.

- Show rows for campaign X in city Y on date Z.
- Top 15 search queries by clicks last week.
- Daily spend and ROAS for the last 14 days.
- Is CPM rising in Keyword Based Ads?
- Compare this week with last week by placement.
- Compare August with July by city.
- What share of GMV came from Collection Ads?
- How many cities did the Troovy campaign run in?
- Which months are loaded?
- Any day with unusual spend?
- Cities where ROAS fell but spend rose.
- Do the three tables agree on search placement totals?
